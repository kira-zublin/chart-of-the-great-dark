# First feature: profiles and characters

The application opens at a sign-in page. A new player or GM enters a profile name, password, shared invitation code, and desired role. Returning users sign in with their name and password. The browser keeps an HTTP-only session cookie for 30 days; logout clears it. Anyone with the invite code may choose GM at registration, as requested for this private group.

After sign-in the existing star map appears. The profile menu in the upper left shows the name and role and offers logout. The upper-right character menu replaces Reset Chart. It offers a list, character creation, and a confirmation before permanent deletion. Player lists show only their own PCs. GM lists show every PC and NPC, with a filter by type. GMs may edit PCs and create and edit NPCs.

A character has a name, profession, origin, faction, appearance, motivation, freeform description, and the six Explorer Sheet attributes: Strength, Agility, Logic, Insight, Perception, and Empathy. It also accepts a portrait, a full stand-up image, and an optional delve-suit stand-up that players can switch to in Vistas. The sheet shows the creation rules as guidance but never blocks an established character.

The map's existing panning, zooming, markers, dossier, and assets remain intact. Character edits save to the server. The browser remembers the selected character locally for convenience, while its fields and images are loaded from persistent storage.

## Expedition console

Characters, the crew, and GM tools share one slide-in panel on the right. Its top tabs are Mapping, Music, Creatures, Characters, and Crew for the GM; players see only Characters and Crew. The upper-right Crew Sheet button opens the Crew tab, choosing or creating a character opens the Characters tab, and the GM-only GM Tools button opens Mapping. Pressing the same button again closes the panel. Mapping holds the location controls that previously floated over the map (pulling characters, fog and rooms, editing and creating locations); selecting a map marker loads it into the edit form. Creatures holds the creature palette: a searchable list of palette entries grouped by category, each opening a stat block the GM can edit, duplicate or delete, plus New creature for the GM's own entries.

Vistas show a visual-novel dialog box that echoes chat lines from the characters in that scene and from the GM, with a portrait and letter-by-letter text. Rolls and out-of-character lines (`//`, `((`, `ooc:`) are left out, and each player can collapse it to a Show dialog button.

The Music tab is the GM's jukebox. The GM uploads a licensed MP3 with a title, sees the library with durations, sizes and total storage, and can play, restart, pause, resume, stop, loop or delete tracks, and set a volume for everyone that scales each player's own volume. One track plays at a time for everyone signed in, and players who join or reload mid-track hear it from the current position. Players never see track titles. Every profile has a Sound control in the top bar with mute and volume, remembered in that browser. When a browser blocks audio until someone interacts with the page, the control changes to a highlighted Enable sound prompt, and any click starts the music. Switching top tabs keeps an unsaved character draft; closing the panel with unsaved character changes asks for confirmation.

## Character and crew sheets

The Characters tab is the Explorer sheet. A header stays in view above its tabs: the portrait medallion (click to replace it), the name with profession, specialty, origin and faction, the Health, Hope and Heart tracks, and chips for active conditions. Maximums come from the attributes (Health = Strength + Agility, Hope = Logic + Empathy, Heart = Insight + Perception). Click a diamond to set the current value; clicking the highest lit diamond takes one point. At zero a track reads Broken.

The four keyboard-accessible tabs are Abilities, Condition, Gear and Profile.

- **Abilities** shows the attribute wheel: the six attributes on a hexagon, ordered so each pool's pair shares an edge, with each condition tag beside the attribute it weakens. Selecting an attribute opens the chat roll window for it, with any condition penalty already applied. Adjust unlocks the rings for editing, shows the 24-point budget, and lets talents be added or changed without spending XP; the profession's key attribute is starred. Outside Adjust, Raise and the talent picker spend XP at the book's cost (5 × the new level). The picker lists the profession's key talents first and accepts talents that aren't in the book.
- **Condition** holds the six condition tiles, and cards for critical injuries (with a lethal flag and healing time), mental trauma and Blight manifestations. Older free-text notes become a single card.
- **Gear** holds Supply and rukh counters, a carry bar against Strength + 4 rows, weapon and armor cards with range bands and feature chips, equipment with a weight (tiny, light, regular or heavy), tiny items, and the keepsake, which restores 1 Hope once per session (per calendar day).
- **Profile** holds origin, faction, profession and specialty as book choices with a write-your-own option. Each choice shows what it grants and can add the granted talent. Motivation, quirk, appearance, contacts and description are free text, shown as inked lines until focused. The image frames upload the portrait and stand-ups.

When the rules reference has been imported, a talent's name opens its description, the talent picker shows the highlighted talent's description, Add injury, Add trauma and Add manifestation offer the book's table (choosing a row fills the card and marks any condition it gives), and feature chips show their rule on hover.

An existing character saves itself shortly after each change; the footer shows Saving…, Saved or the error. A new character opens on Profile in Adjust mode and is saved with Create character. Closing the panel or switching character saves any pending change first. With no character open, the tab offers to create one.

The Crew Sheet is the Crew tab of the console, available to every signed-in profile. Its five tabs are Crew Info, Maneuvers, Bird, Rover, and Shuttle. Crew Info includes the crew portrait, name, points, and five role slots. Maneuvers are separate addable rows with a name and multi-line description. Existing single-line maneuvers remain available as names. Bird includes a portrait and multi-line appearance, description, and Powers; Rover and Shuttle have multi-line cargo. Players can place their own PCs in an empty slot or remove their own assignment; the GM can manage any PC. Every shared field saves on change. The panel refreshes during play and reports conflicts rather than silently replacing another person's edit.
